# What We Do

## From Search
## To Shopping Cart.

### Product Page SEO

Your product pages need to do two jobs:

**Get discovered.**

**Get chosen.**

We optimise product titles, descriptions, headings, metadata, content, images, internal links, and other on-page elements to improve search relevance and user experience.

### Category Page SEO

Category pages can capture valuable high-intent searches.

We optimise category structures and content to create stronger opportunities for your important product groups to rank.

### E-commerce Keyword Strategy

We identify search opportunities across:

**Products**

**Categories**

**Brands**

**Features**

**Use Cases**

**Long-Tail Searches**

Then map those opportunities to the pages most likely to satisfy the search intent.

### Technical E-commerce SEO

Large online stores can develop complex technical problems.

We look for issues such as:

**Duplicate URLs**

**Indexation problems**

**Faceted navigation**

**Broken links**

**Redirects**

**Canonicalisation**

**Site architecture**

**XML sitemaps**

The goal is to help search engines efficiently discover and understand your store.

### Internal Linking

Your products and categories should connect logically.

We create smarter internal linking opportunities to help users discover relevant products while helping search engines understand your site's structure.

### Content Strategy

Not every customer starts with a product search.

Some start with a question.

Some start with a problem.

Some are researching before buying.

We help identify content opportunities that can bring potential customers into your ecosystem **before they're ready to purchase.**

### Image & Product Optimisation

Search isn't only about text.

We consider product imagery, image optimisation, alt text, page performance, and other elements that contribute to a better shopping and search experience.

### SEO for New Products

Launching something new?

We can build search-ready product pages and supporting content from the beginning instead of waiting for visibility problems later.

### E-commerce SEO Audit

Already have an online store?

We'll look for technical issues, content gaps, keyword opportunities, structural problems, and pages that aren't reaching their potential.
