# What We Do

## From Technical Foundations
## To Search Relevance.

### Technical SEO

We identify and address technical issues that can make it harder for search engines to crawl and understand your website.

We look at areas such as:

**Crawlability**

**Indexation**

**Site Structure**

**Page Speed**

**Broken Links**

**Redirects**

**Canonicalisation**

**XML Sitemaps**

**Robots.txt**

### On-Page SEO

Every important page should have a clear purpose.

We optimise page elements to make that purpose easier for both search engines and users to understand.

This can include:

**Title Tags**

**Meta Descriptions**

**Headings**

**Content Structure**

**Keywords**

**Internal Links**

**Image Alt Text**

**URL Structure**

### Keyword & Search Intent

Ranking isn't about putting keywords everywhere.

It's about understanding **what someone actually wants when they search.**

We map relevant keywords and search intent to the right pages so your content has a clearer opportunity to rank.

### Internal Linking

Your pages shouldn't exist in isolation.

We build smarter internal linking structures that help users navigate your website and help search engines discover relationships between important pages.

### Content Optimisation

Already have content?

You may not need to start from scratch.

We analyse existing pages and identify opportunities to improve relevance, structure, clarity, depth, and search intent alignment.

### Mobile & Performance

Your customers aren't always searching from a desktop.

We consider mobile experience and performance as part of the wider optimisation process.

Because a slow or difficult website can lose visitors before they even become customers.
